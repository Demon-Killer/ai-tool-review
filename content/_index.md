AI Tool Radar covers AI tools for developers, indie builders, and small technical teams. We focus on four things that actually matter when choosing a tool: pricing reality (including free-tier limits and hidden costs), workflow fit, honest limitations, and ecosystem maturity.

## What We Do Differently

Most AI tool review sites recycle vendor marketing copy. We do not. Every review here is built from:

- **Verified pricing** checked against each vendor's official pricing page at the time of writing
- **Community signal** aggregated from Reddit, Hacker News, GitHub issues, and developer forums
- **Comparative analysis** that maps each tool against alternatives on the same axes
- **Critical assessment** that flags overhyped features and gaps between marketing claims and real-world behavior

We do not invent testing data. We do not accept payment for positive reviews. We do not have affiliate links. The business model is display advertising only — see our [Affiliate Disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/) for the full picture.

## What We Cover

Our coverage is deliberately narrow. We focus on tools that matter to technical teams:

- **AI coding assistants** — Copilot, Cursor, Claude Code, and alternatives
- **AI automation tools** — Zapier, Make, n8n, and workflow orchestration
- **AI writing tools** — for technical content, documentation, and communication
- **AI data analysis tools** — for working with datasets and code
- **Developer-adjacent AI tools** — APIs, voice, transcription, search

We do not cover health, medical, financial, or legal AI tools. Those require domain expertise we do not have.

## How We Work

Our research process is documented in our [Editorial Policy](/editorial-policy/). The short version: every claim is sourced, every price is verified, and every correction is logged at the bottom of the affected article.

When we make hands-on testing claims, those articles include the testing date, the plan used, the specific tasks attempted, and what worked versus what failed. Articles without those elements are research-based reviews — we label the difference clearly so you know what you are reading.

If you find outdated pricing or a factual error, email us at **lidonson666@gmail.com**. We investigate corrections within 48 hours.
