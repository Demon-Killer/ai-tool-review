## About AI Tool Radar

AI Tool Radar is an independent review site dedicated to helping you find the best AI tools for your needs. We cut through marketing claims and surface-level feature lists to give you honest, practical assessments.

### What Makes Us Different

Most AI tool review sites recycle the same surface-level information. We do the work others skip:

- **Verified pricing from official sources** — every price we cite is checked against the vendor's current pricing page, not copied from outdated third-party articles
- **Honest limitations** — we tell you where each tool falls short, not just where it shines
- **Decision frameworks** — instead of vague recommendations, we give you specific criteria for choosing based on your situation
- **No fabricated benchmarks** — we do not invent test data or make up hands-on testing narratives. When we reference community findings or academic studies, we cite sources

### Our Research Process

1. **Source verification**: We check each tool's official pricing page, documentation, and API references
2. **Community signal**: We aggregate feedback from Reddit, Hacker News, and developer forums to understand real-world usage
3. **Comparative analysis**: We map each tool against alternatives on the same axes (price, features, limitations, best-fit use cases)
4. **Critical assessment**: We flag where tools are overhyped, underperforming, or poorly documented
5. **Source citations**: Where data comes from external sources, we link to them so you can verify

### Why We Do Not Claim "Hands-On Testing"

You will notice we do not say "we tested this tool for 30 days" on most reviews. That is deliberate. Fabricating testing narratives — inventing latency numbers, response times, or comparison scores without actually running the tests — is common in this niche and we refuse to do it.

When we make a claim about a tool's behavior, it is grounded in one of:

- The vendor's own published specifications (clearly marked as such)
- Documented community testing (linked)
- General architectural facts (e.g., "self-hosted means no per-call cost")

If a claim is uncertain, we say so. If we have not verified something, we do not state it as fact.

### Corrections and Updates

AI tools change fast — pricing shifts, features get added or deprecated, vendors get acquired. If you find outdated information in one of our reviews, email us and we will update it within 48 hours.

### Editorial Independence

We do not accept payment for positive reviews. We do not have affiliate relationships with the tools we review. If that ever changes, we will disclose it here.
