Everyone makes writing mistakes. AI grammar checkers catch errors, suggest improvements, and help you write more clearly. Here are the best options in 2026.

## 1. Grammarly

**Best for: The most comprehensive AI writing assistant**

Grammarly is the most popular AI grammar checker, and for good reason. It catches more errors than any competitor and provides detailed explanations for each suggestion.

**Key features:**
- Grammar, spelling, and punctuation checking
- Tone detection and adjustment
- Style and clarity suggestions
- Plagiarism detection (Premium)
- Works everywhere (browser extension, desktop, mobile)
- AI-powered sentence rewriting
- Genre-specific writing style detection

**Pros:**
- Catches the most errors across all categories
- Works in Gmail, Google Docs, Word, social media, and more
- Clear explanations help you learn from mistakes
- Free version covers grammar and spelling well
- Tone detector helps match your audience

**Cons:**
- Premium suggestions can feel prescriptive
- Sometimes flags correct but uncommon phrasing
- Premium is $12/month
- Can slow down browser with heavy use

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Premium at $12/month. Business at $15/month.

**Our take:** Grammarly is the best grammar checker for most people. Install the browser extension and it improves everything you write online.

## 2. ProWritingAid

**Best for: Authors and long-form writers**

ProWritingAid goes beyond grammar checking to analyze your entire writing style. It provides detailed reports on readability, pacing, repetition, and more.

**Key features:**
- 20+ writing analysis reports
- Grammar and style checking
- Readability analysis
- Pacing and structure feedback
- Repetition and overused word detection
- Integrates with Scrivener, Word, and Google Docs
- AI-powered sentence suggestions

**Pros:**
- Best analysis for long-form writing
- Detailed reports teach you to write better
- Good value (lifetime plan available)
- Integrates with writing software

**Cons:**
- Interface less polished than Grammarly
- Can be overwhelming with too many reports
- Free tier very limited (500 word limit)

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Premium at $13/month. Lifetime at $399.

**Our take:** If you write books, long articles, or academic papers, ProWritingAid provides deeper analysis than Grammarly.

## 3. LanguageTool

**Best for: Multi-language writing and privacy**

LanguageTool is an open-source grammar checker that supports 30+ languages. It can be self-hosted for complete privacy.

**Key features:**
- Supports 30+ languages
- Open-source core
- Self-hostable for privacy
- Browser extension and desktop app
- Style suggestions
- Finds errors that other tools miss in non-English languages

**Pros:**
- Best multi-language support
- Open source (transparent)
- Can run locally (complete privacy)
- Good free tier
- More affordable than Grammarly

**Cons:**
- Fewer features than Grammarly
- English checking slightly less accurate
- Less polished interface
- Smaller user community

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Premium at $5/month.

**Our take:** LanguageTool is the best choice for non-English writing or anyone who values privacy and open source.

## 4. Quillbot

**Best for: Paraphrasing and rewriting sentences**

Quillbot specializes in rewriting and paraphrasing text. It helps you say the same thing in different ways, which is useful for avoiding repetition and improving clarity.

**Key features:**
- AI-powered paraphrasing
- Multiple rewriting modes (Standard, Formal, Creative, etc.)
- Grammar checker
- Summarizer
- Citation generator
- Translator

**Pros:**
- Excellent at rephrasing sentences
- Multiple tone/style options
- Good for breaking writer's block
- Free tier available

**Cons:**
- Paraphrasing can change meaning unintentionally
- Grammar checking less comprehensive than Grammarly
- Free tier limits paraphrasing length

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Premium at $10/month.

## FAQ

### Do I really need a grammar checker?
If you write emails, social media posts, documents, or anything that others read, yes. Grammarly's free tier alone catches mistakes you would miss.

### Is Grammarly worth paying for?
If you write professionally or frequently, yes. Premium catches style issues, tone problems, and clarity issues that the free tier misses. For casual use, the free tier is enough.

### Can AI grammar checkers replace human editors?
For everyday writing, they are sufficient. For published books, academic papers, or important business documents, a human editor still provides value that AI cannot match.

### Which grammar checker is most accurate for English?
Grammarly. It consistently catches the most errors and provides the most helpful suggestions for English writing.

## Bottom Line

**Grammarly** is the best grammar checker for most people. Start with the free browser extension. **LanguageTool** if you write in multiple languages or care about privacy. **ProWritingAid** if you write books or long-form content.
