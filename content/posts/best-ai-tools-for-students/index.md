## Research: Perplexity AI

Perplexity is an AI search engine that provides sourced answers. For academic research, it is more useful than ChatGPT because it cites its sources.

**Pricing:**
- Free: 5 Pro searches/day, basic answers
- Pro: $20/month (unlimited Pro search, 20 Deep Research queries/day, premium models)

**How to use Perplexity for research:**
```
"What are the main arguments for and against [topic] in
recent academic literature? Include citations to specific
papers or authors where possible."
```

**Important limitation:** AI research tools can hallucinate citations. Always verify that cited papers actually exist and say what the AI claims they say. Use Perplexity to find starting points, then verify through your university library or Google Scholar.

## Writing: ChatGPT and Claude

### For Outlines and Structure
```
"Create a detailed outline for a [type] paper on [topic].
Include: introduction with thesis statement, main arguments
with supporting evidence sections, counterarguments, and
conclusion. Format for a [length] paper."
```

### For Grammar and Style
```
"Review this paragraph for: grammar errors, unclear phrasing,
passive voice, and wordiness. Suggest improvements while
maintaining my original meaning and voice."
```

### For Citations (Use With Caution)
AI can format citations but sometimes generates incorrect citation details. Always verify journal names, page numbers, and publication dates manually.

**Responsible use:** AI can help with structure, grammar, and brainstorming. Submitting AI-generated text as your own work violates academic integrity policies at most institutions. Use AI to improve your thinking and writing, not to replace it.

## Math and Problem-Solving: ChatGPT

ChatGPT handles math explanations well when you ask for step-by-step solutions.

**Effective math prompt:**
```
"Solve this problem step by step, explaining each step:
[paste problem]. After solving, explain the concept
so I can solve similar problems on my own."
```

**What ChatGPT handles well:**
- Algebra, calculus, statistics explanations
- Step-by-step solutions with reasoning
- Explaining why a particular method works

**Where ChatGPT struggles:**
- Complex multi-step proofs
- Problems requiring visual reasoning (geometry diagrams)
- Advanced graduate-level mathematics
- Can make calculation errors — always verify

## Coding Assignments: GitHub Copilot Free

GitHub Copilot Free provides 2,000 code suggestions per month at no cost. It integrates into VS Code and helps with:

- Autocompleting code as you type
- Explaining what code does
- Suggesting fixes for errors
- Generating boilerplate code

**Responsible use:** Use Copilot to learn and understand code, not to generate complete assignments. Understanding the code you submit is essential for exams and interviews.

## Exam Preparation

**Generate practice questions:**
```
"Create 20 practice questions about [topic] at [level:
introductory/intermediate/advanced]. Include a mix of
multiple choice, short answer, and essay questions.
Provide answers and explanations for each."
```

**Review concepts:**
```
"Explain [concept] as if teaching a student who is
struggling. Use simple language, analogies, and
2-3 examples. Then give me 5 practice problems
to test my understanding."
```

**Create study schedule:**
```
"I have an exam on [date] covering [topics].
Create a study schedule starting today. Include:
daily topics, review sessions, and practice test days.
I can study [hours] per day."
```

## Academic Integrity Guidelines

AI tools are powerful but their use must be transparent:

1. **Check your institution's AI policy.** Most universities have specific guidelines on what AI use is permitted.
2. **Use AI for learning, not output.** AI helps you understand and practice. Submitting AI-generated work as your own violates academic integrity.
3. **Cite AI assistance when required.** Some institutions require disclosing AI tool usage.
4. **Verify everything.** AI can produce plausible-sounding but incorrect information, fake citations, and wrong math. You are responsible for accuracy.

## FAQ

### Can I use ChatGPT for homework?
Check your institution's policy. Most allow AI for brainstorming, outlining, and learning concepts. Most prohibit submitting AI-generated text as your own work.

### Which AI tool is best for math?
ChatGPT handles most undergraduate math well when you ask for step-by-step explanations. For advanced math, Wolfram Alpha (free tier) provides more reliable computation.

### Is Perplexity better than Google for academic research?
For finding specific information quickly, yes — Perplexity provides sourced, summarized answers. For comprehensive literature reviews, Google Scholar and your university library remain essential. Use Perplexity as a starting point, not your only research tool.

### Do I need to pay for any AI tools as a student?
No. The free tiers of ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, GitHub Copilot, and Grammarly cover all academic needs. Many tools also offer student discounts if you want to upgrade.
