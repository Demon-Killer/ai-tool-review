## 1. ChatGPT / Claude - Versatile Email Drafting

**Best for: Any type of email**

The simplest approach is to use a general AI assistant for email writing. Describe what you need, and it generates a draft.

**How to use:**
- "Write a professional email declining a meeting invitation"
- "Draft a follow-up email after a job interview"
- "Write a friendly reminder about an overdue invoice"
- "Create a cold outreach email for my web design services"

**Pros:**
- Handles any email type
- Can adjust tone (formal, casual, persuasive)
- Free tier works well
- Can rewrite existing emails

**Cons:**
- Requires copy/pasting between tools
- No direct email integration
- Needs context about the situation

**Pro tip:** Create a template prompt for recurring emails. For example:
```
Write a professional follow-up email. Context: [situation].
Tone: friendly but professional. Keep it under 100 words.
Include a clear call to action.
```

## 2. Grammarly - Real-time Email Enhancement

**Best for: Polishing emails as you write**

Grammarly works inside your email client (Gmail, Outlook, etc.) and improves your writing in real-time.

**Pros:**
- Works directly in Gmail, Outlook, and other clients
- Catches grammar and spelling errors instantly
- Tone detector ensures you sound right
- Suggests clarity improvements
- Free version covers the basics

**Cons:**
- Not a full email generator
- Premium suggestions can be overly formal
- Tone suggestions sometimes miss context

**Pricing:** Free tier available. Premium at $12/month.

## 3. Superhuman - AI Email Client

**Best for: Email power users**

Superhuman is a premium email client that uses AI throughout the email experience. It is designed for people who live in their inbox.

**AI features:**
- AI drafts replies based on your writing style
- Auto-summarizes long email threads
- Smart routing and prioritization
- Keyboard shortcuts for speed
- Follow-up reminders
- Read receipts

**Pros:**
- Learns your writing style over time
- Fastest email experience available
- Beautiful, distraction-free interface
- AI-generated replies save significant time

**Cons:**
- Expensive at $30/month
- Waitlist or invitation required sometimes
- Only works with Gmail and Outlook
- Learning curve for keyboard shortcuts

**Pricing:** $30/month.

**Our take:** If email is a core part of your job and you spend 3+ hours daily in your inbox, Superhuman pays for itself in time saved.

## 4. Lavender - Sales Email Optimization

**Best for: Sales teams and cold outreach**

Lavender is specifically designed for sales emails. It scores your emails, suggests improvements, and helps you write emails that get replies.

**AI features:**
- Email scoring (rates your email on how likely it is to get a reply)
- AI suggestions for improving subject lines and body
- Prospect research integration (shows you info about the recipient)
- A/B testing recommendations
- Integration with Gmail, Outlook, and Sales tools

**Pros:**
- Specifically optimized for sales emails
- Data-driven scoring system
- Integrates with CRM tools
- Free tier for individuals

**Cons:**
- Focused on sales use cases only
- Team plans get expensive
- Limited value for non-sales emails

**Pricing:** Free tier for individuals. Starter at $29/month.

## 5. Microsoft Copilot for Outlook

**Best for: Outlook users in corporate environments**

If you use Outlook at work, Microsoft Copilot integrates AI directly into your email workflow.

**AI features:**
- Draft emails from brief instructions
- Summarize long email threads
- Suggest replies based on context
- Pull information from other emails and documents
- Meeting scheduling assistance

**Pros:**
- Seamless Outlook integration
- Access to your organization's data
- Enterprise-grade security
- No new tool to learn

**Cons:**
- Requires Microsoft 365 Copilot subscription
- Corporate environments only
- Quality varies by use case

**Pricing:** Microsoft 365 Copilot at $30/user/month.

## Email Writing Tips with AI

### 1. Provide Good Context
The more context you give, the better the output:
```
Bad: "Write a follow-up email"
Good: "Write a follow-up email to a potential client (John at Acme Corp)
who I met at a conference last week. We discussed their need for a new
website. I want to schedule a 30-minute call to discuss next steps."
```

### 2. Specify Tone and Length
```
"Write this in a warm but professional tone, keep it under 150 words"
```

### 3. Edit, Don't Just Send
Always review AI-generated emails before sending. Check for:
- Accuracy of names, dates, and details
- Appropriate tone for the relationship
- Any information you did not intend to share

### 4. Create Templates for Recurring Emails
Save prompts for emails you write frequently:
- Follow-ups after meetings
- Project status updates
- Client onboarding messages
- Invoice reminders

## FAQ

### Can AI write professional emails?
Yes, very well. AI generates professional, well-structured emails that often need minimal editing. The key is providing good context.

### Will people know my email was written by AI?
Usually not, if you edit the output. AI-generated emails can sound generic if sent without review, but with minor personalization they are indistinguishable from human-written ones.

### Which tool is best for cold emails?
Lavender for sales outreach (it optimizes for reply rates). ChatGPT or Claude for general cold emails (they handle varied contexts well).

### Is it ethical to use AI for email?
Yes. AI is a writing tool, similar to using spell check or templates. You are responsible for the content and accuracy of what you send.

## Bottom Line

For most people, the best setup is **ChatGPT or Claude for drafting + Grammarly for polishing**. Both have free tiers that work well. Upgrade to **Superhuman** if email is a major part of your workday.
